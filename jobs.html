Job Title: Social Media Manager

Reference number: SCM482

Salary: $75,000 - $90,000 per year

Position Description

The Social Media Manager will manage the organisation's online presence and promote environmental conservation projects, community campaigns, educational resources and volunteer opportunities across social media platforms.

Reporting Line

Reports to the Communications and Marketing Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and manage social media content across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and other relevant platforms.
  • Create engaging posts, videos, graphics and stories promoting conservation activities.
  • Manage the organisation's social media calendar and publishing schedule.
  • Monitor audience engagement, comments and messages.
  • Promote volunteer opportunities, fundraising campaigns and community events.
  • Track social media performance and prepare regular reports.
  • Work with conservation staff to communicate project outcomes and environmental issues.

Essential Requirements

  1. Qualification or relevant experience in marketing, communications or social media management.
  2. Demonstrated experience managing professional social media accounts.
  3. Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  4. Good understanding of social media platforms and digital content creation.
  5. Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
  6. Genuine interest in environmental conservation and sustainability.

Preferred Skills

  • Experience working for a not-for-profit or environmental organisation.
  • Experience with photography, video editing or graphic design.
  • Knowledge of social media analytics and advertising platforms.
  • Experience promoting fundraising or community campaigns.

Job Title: Sustainability Educator

Reference number: SUE719

Salary: $70,000 - $85,000 per year

Position Description

The Sustainability Educator will develop and deliver educational programs that help schools, community groups and the public understand sustainability, environmental protection and responsible resource use.

Reporting Line

Reports to the Education and Community Programs Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop educational workshops and sustainability learning resources.
  • Deliver presentations and activities to schools and community groups.
  • Educate participants about recycling, biodiversity, climate change and sustainable living.
  • Organise environmental awareness events and community education sessions.
  • Create engaging educational materials for different age groups.
  • Work with teachers, community organisations and conservation staff.
  • Evaluate educational programs and recommend improvements.

Essential Requirements

  1. Qualification or experience in environmental education, sustainability, teaching or a related field.
  2. Strong knowledge of environmental and sustainability principles.
  3. Excellent presentation and communication skills.
  4. Ability to explain environmental topics clearly to different audiences.
  5. Strong organisational and time-management skills.
  6. Ability to work with schools, community groups and volunteers.

Preferred Skills

  • Teaching or environmental education experience.
  • Experience developing educational resources or workshops.
  • Knowledge of local Australian ecosystems and conservation issues.
  • First aid certification.
  • Experience working with children or community groups.

Job Title: Volunteer Opportunities Coordinator

Reference number: VOC365

Salary: $65,000 - $78,000 per year

Position Description

The Volunteer Opportunities Coordinator will recruit, support and coordinate volunteers involved in conservation projects, community campaigns, environmental events and fundraising activities.

Reporting Line

Reports to the Volunteer and Community Engagement Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Recruit and welcome new volunteers to the organisation.
  • Coordinate volunteer opportunities across conservation and community projects.
  • Maintain volunteer records, schedules and contact information.
  • Organise volunteer induction and training sessions.
  • Communicate regularly with volunteers about upcoming activities.
  • Support conservation teams during volunteer events.
  • Recognise volunteer contributions and encourage ongoing participation.
  • Collect feedback from volunteers and improve volunteer programs.

Essential Requirements

  1. Experience in volunteer coordination, community engagement, administration or a related field.
  2. Excellent interpersonal and communication skills.
  3. Strong organisational and scheduling abilities.
  4. Ability to work with people from diverse backgrounds.
  5. Good computer and administrative skills.
  6. Genuine interest in environmental conservation and community participation.

Preferred Skills

  • Previous experience working with environmental or not-for-profit organisations.
  • Experience coordinating community events or volunteer programs.
  • Knowledge of workplace health and safety procedures.
  • Experience using volunteer management databases.
  • First aid certification or experience working in outdoor environments.
